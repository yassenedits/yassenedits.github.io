About

1 / EDITOR

I'm Yassen, a freelance video editor based in Alexandria with two years of hands-on experience cutting reels, ads, and long-form video for brands and creators across Egypt.

I turn raw footage into clean, well-paced edits that keep people watching, from fast social cuts to polished promos. I work in CapCut and DaVinci Resolve and take care of the pacing, motion, and color, so all you need to send me is your footage and your deadline. And since I'm studying at the College of Agriculture, I know that good work takes patience and the right conditions.
